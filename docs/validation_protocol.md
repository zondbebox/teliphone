# Proposed real-user validation protocol

**Research status: not conducted.** This is a recruitment and testing plan, not participant evidence. Automated and agent-driven browser checks establish software behavior only; they do not substitute for real-user research. No respondents have been contacted by this task.

## Participants and recruitment

Propose ten adult prospective travelers who reside in India, can comfortably complete an English booking task and are considering a Kazakhstan trip within the next 12 months. Seek a mix of couples and small friend groups, first-time and experienced international travelers, and phone/laptop users. Confirm interest through screening; do not recruit solely friends who already know the prototype. Separately consult two operator staff or guides if available; record them as operational informants, not additional target-traveler successes.

Recruitment is a future human-run activity through consented travel communities or operator contacts. Use a neutral invitation describing a 25-35 minute prototype test, its fictional company case, no purchase requirement and any actual incentive. Do not invent incentives or spend a recruitment budget without authorization. If only fewer participants attend, report the true number. Ten is an optional case enhancement and a small formative sample, not statistical proof of a market effect.

## Consent and handling

Explain purpose, voluntary participation, the ability to stop, which observations will be recorded and how long data will be kept. Obtain separate explicit consent for recording. Use anonymous participant codes; store consent separately. Request no card numbers, passport details, passwords or real booking. Use provided synthetic names/emails for the demo. Proposed retention: delete identifiable raw recordings within 30 days after analysis and retain anonymized task notes only if consent permits; this policy requires team approval and appropriate legal review. If any minors participate, use the applicable guardian/school safeguards rather than assuming adult consent rules suffice.

## Moderator script and tasks

Say: "We are testing the design, not your skill. This is a fictional company's prototype. Please think aloud. You will not purchase a tour or enter payment-card details." Do not show the happy path first. Ask clarifying questions after a task rather than directing clicks during it. Mark any assistance.

| Task | Prompt | Observe |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Find a fit | Using the supplied date, party size, budget, starting city and difficulty, find a suitable departure. | Match accuracy, ability to understand an honest no-match result, time and assistance |
| 2. Understand the offer | Explain two included services, two exclusions, meeting location and physical demands to a travel companion. | Correct answers against current Trip Truth Card; missed qualifiers |
| 3. Price and cancellation | State the per-person charge, complete party total in KZT and the cancellation consequence for the supplied scenario. | Correct total/currency; misunderstanding exchange fees or demo terms |
| 4. Book safely | Use synthetic traveler details, review terms and complete the labelled successful payment simulation. | Unassisted completion, invalid input recovery, recognition that no money moved |
| 5. Recover from decline | Try the provided declined-payment scenario and explain the next step. | No loss of entered information, appropriate retry, no belief that payment succeeded |
| 6. Pending/cancelled | Inspect a pending then a separate cancelled example and say whether a booking is confirmed. | Correct state interpretation; no duplicate booking attempt |
| 7. Trip Passport | Find the reference, meeting instructions and guide-assignment status. | Correct interpretation of unassigned guide; retrieval without help |
| 8. Sold out | Try the specified full departure and choose a safe next step. | No accidental booking, clear alternative/no-match understanding |

Operational staff tasks: update content and departure capacity, identify a follow-up question, assign a guide and check the traveler-visible result. Record time, errors and any need for a spreadsheet workaround.

## Measures and proposed decision thresholds

These thresholds are planning choices, not measured results. Fix them before conducting sessions.

| Measure | Definition | Proposed pilot gate |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Core completion | Tasks 1 and 4 completed correctly without moderator intervention | At least 8/10 participants |
| Price comprehension | Correct party total and currency before confirming | At least 9/10 |
| Inclusion comprehension | Correctly identifies two exclusions and relevant cancellation rule | At least 9/10 |
| Payment-state comprehension | Correctly distinguishes declined/pending/cancelled from confirmed | 10/10 on safety-critical distinction |
| Decline recovery | Finds safe retry/support path without claiming a successful charge | At least 8/10 |
| Critical errors | Wrong total, false confirmation, overbooking, or disclosure of sensitive data | Zero; fix and retest before pilot |
| Accessibility observation | Keyboard/focus/zoom and small-screen tasks complete without blocking content | Zero unresolved blocking issues |
| Confidence | Self-rated 1-5 confidence and specific reason | Report distribution and quotes with consent; no invented uplift |

Record each task's success, assistance, time, error and participant explanation in a blank observation sheet. Report numerators and denominators, not only percentages. A small non-random sample cannot establish conversion uplift or nationally representative preferences.

## Interview questions

1. How did you first hear about Kazakhstan, and where would you look for a short guided trip?
2. What information would you need before trusting an unfamiliar tour operator?
3. Which details were unclear on this tour page, and what would you ask a person?
4. What do you believe the total covers, and what would you still need to pay for?
5. What would you expect after a card decline or a pending result?
6. Which language would you choose for this decision? What English terms were difficult?
7. Does the meeting city and trip length fit your real plans? Why or why not?
8. What would prevent you from booking, even if the interface worked?

Ask about past experiences without requesting sensitive financial data. Do not lead with "Would our trustworthy AI improve your booking?" or interpret politeness as purchase intent.

## Twelve-week placement and decision rules

Weeks 1-2: team/operator review and consent/recruitment preparation; benchmark a configured tool. Weeks 3-4: early comprehension sessions on the card and flow. Weeks 7-8: payment-state and security testing with synthetic data. Weeks 9-10: recruit and conduct the target-user sessions. Weeks 11-12: fix critical issues and retest failed tasks before a limited authorized pilot. Payment onboarding and operational capacity are separate go/no-go gates.

For commercial validation after launch, compare incremental completed travelers and contribution by acquisition cohort, accounting for cancellations, service delivery, payment cost and marketing. Track online migration separately. Do not convert test-task completion into a financial conversion-rate assumption without actual traffic and completion evidence.
