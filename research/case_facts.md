## Calculations from those facts, not new case facts

- 3,400 x 44,000 = 149,600,000 KZT, which is 400,000 KZT below the stated revenue (about 0.27%). Preserve both supplied values; the case does not explain the discrepancy.
- An approximate 4% foreign share implies roughly 136 foreign and 3,264 domestic travelers at the 2025 baseline. The 96% figure is approximate, so these are planning estimates.
- If domestic travelers remain 3,264 per year, a 30% annual foreign share needs at least about 1,399 foreign travelers per year: 0.30 x 3,264 / 0.70 = 1,398.86. About 1,263 would be additional to the approximate 136 baseline. A report's exact forecast must come from `finance/`, not this arithmetic alone.

## Four categories used in this package

1. **Case fact:** the fictional company's given facts above.
2. **Externally verified fact:** opened primary-source evidence logged in `sources.csv` with year, unit, access date and limitation.
3. **Proposed assumption:** market choice, customer persona, demo tour details, capacity, costs, acquisition conversion and contribution margins that have not been observed or accepted by the team.
4. **Calculated projection:** formulas applied to stated assumptions. A projection is not a statistic or a promised result.

No interviews, surveys, real payments, real bookings, provider sandbox transactions, team approvals, school details or guide qualifications are established by these notes.
